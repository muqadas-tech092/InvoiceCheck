🧾 InvoiceCheck

Catch wrong totals, duplicate invoices and missing details before they reach your books. Everything runs in your browser: your invoices are never uploaded.

Pattern-based extraction: it handles common invoice layouts, flags anything it is unsure about, and never guesses silently. Always review flagged invoices. Scanned (image-only) PDFs need OCR, which this tool does not do. This is not accounting advice. Sample invoices are fictional. PDF reading uses PDF.js (Apache-2.0), bundled with the site.
